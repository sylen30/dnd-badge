⚔ D&D Badge

Flash the latest badge firmware straight from your browser.

Your browser can't do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page needs to be opened over HTTPS.
  1. Plug the badge in with a USB data cable.
  2. Click Connect & flash and pick the badge's serial port.
  3. Choose Install. Tick Erase device for a clean wipe.
  4. Wait for it to finish, then unplug. Next badge!

If no port shows up, you may need the CP210x or CH340 USB driver, or hold the BOOT button while clicking connect.
